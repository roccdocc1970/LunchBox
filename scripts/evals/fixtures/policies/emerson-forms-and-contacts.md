# ⚡ Emerson Forms and Contacts

This page lists the forms families need most often and the people to call. It complements the Emerson Parent Handbook.

## Early pickup (Form PU-7)

To collect a student before 3:30 PM, a parent or guardian must complete Form PU-7 (Early Pickup Authorization) at the front office. The form must be completed in person with photo ID. Phone requests for early pickup are not accepted.

## Medication permission (Form MED-3)

Form MED-3 (Medication Permission) must be on file with the school nurse before any medication is given at school. Forms are renewed every August and whenever a prescription changes.

## Records requests (Form RR-2)

Transcript and records requests use Form RR-2. Requests are processed within 5 business days. Families will be notified by email when the records are ready.

## Who to call

Front office: extension 4401. School nurse: extension 4417. Registrar Ms. Alvarez: extension 4430. Facilities and building requests: extension 4455. After hours, leave a message at the front office extension and it will be returned the next school day.
