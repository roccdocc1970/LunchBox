# ⚡ Field Trip Policy

This policy covers school-sponsored trips away from campus.

## Permission slips

Every field trip needs a signed permission slip. Slips must be returned to the front office at least 3 school days before the trip. Students without a signed slip stay at school with another class.

## Chaperones

Each trip needs one adult chaperone for every 8 students. Chaperones must complete a background check before their first trip and sign in at the front office on the morning of the trip.

## Trip costs

The school covers transportation. Families are asked to contribute up to $15 per trip toward admission fees; no student is excluded for inability to pay.
