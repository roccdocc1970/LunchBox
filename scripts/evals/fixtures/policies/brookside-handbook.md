# ⚡ Brookside Parent Handbook

This handbook is for families at Brookside Academy.

## Late pickup

Students not collected by 3:30 PM are charged a late pickup fee of $5 for every 10 minutes after 3:30 PM. Families who expect to be late must email the office.

## Weather closings

Closing decisions are announced by 7:00 AM by email only.

## Dress code

Brookside students may wear any clothing that is clean and modest. There is no uniform.
