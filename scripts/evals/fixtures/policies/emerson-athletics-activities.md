# ⚡ Emerson Athletics and Activities Packet

This packet covers sports, clubs and the swim program. It complements the Emerson Parent Handbook and the Forms and Contacts page.

## Activity permission (Form AP-7)

Form AP-7 (Activity Permission) must be signed by a parent or guardian before a student joins any after-school club or sports team. A new form is needed each season. Forms are returned to the athletics office.

## Pool use (Form PU-9)

Students in the swim program need Form PU-9 (Pool Use Agreement) on file before their first practice. The form includes a swimming-ability check and must be turned in by September 15. Students without it may watch but not swim.

## Pool schedule (Form PU-17)

Form PU-17 (Pool Schedule Change) is used when a family needs to move a child to a different practice lane or time. Changes take effect the following Monday.

## Activity fee waiver (Form FA-12)

Families who cannot afford activity fees may request a waiver with Form FA-12. Waiver decisions are made within 10 business days. Requests are confidential.

## Equipment return (Form ER-3)

Uniforms and borrowed equipment are returned with Form ER-3 at the end of each season. Unreturned equipment is billed at replacement cost.
